### Matting method

Checkerboard/paper-frill background removed by border-connected flood fill
keyed on two learned checker tones plus bright low-chroma "paper" tones
(min >= 176, chroma span <= 36, escalation if loose junk remains). Edge AA
receives a 1px feather plus nearest-background color decontamination. Every matte
re-verified visually over magenta/white composites and 2x edge zooms
(`pipeline/debug/contact-over-magenta.png`, `contact-over-white.png`,
`edge-zoom-check.png`). Final QA: zero checker-parity leftovers; zero junk
islands across all 24 matted outputs (17 singles plus the decoration sheet
and its 6 derived crops).

### Size convention decision (Phase 2.2)

`Kids assets/assets.txt` uses `"size": "1920x1080"` — pixel dimensions, not
bytes. Per your spec, `size` in the Math Quest manifest means **output file
size in bytes**; the legacy dimension convention is preserved under the
explicit key `dimensions` ("1536x1024" etc.). This is stated inside
`manifest.json` -> `meta.conventions`.
